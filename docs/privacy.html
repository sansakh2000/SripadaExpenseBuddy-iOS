Privacy Policy

Sripada Expense Buddy  ·  Effective date: August 2, 2026

TL;DR

Sripada Expense Buddy stores all your data on your device only. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal financial data. The app works fully offline.

1. Information We Collect

Sripada Expense Buddy does not collect personal information. All data you enter — transactions, lend/borrow records, recurring items — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's SwiftData framework and never leaves your device.

2. Microphone & Speech Recognition

The Voice Entry feature uses your device microphone and Apple's on-device Speech Recognition framework (SFSpeechRecognizer). When you tap the mic button:

  • Audio is processed by Apple's Speech framework (locale: en-IN).
  • Apple may send audio to their servers for recognition, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • We never receive, store, or process your audio data.
  • Microphone access is only active while you are using the Voice Entry screen.

You can revoke microphone permission at any time in Settings → Privacy → Microphone → Sripada Expense.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Sripada Expense Buddy offers optional Pro subscriptions (monthly / annual) via Apple's StoreKit framework. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Subscription management is handled through your Apple ID account.

4. Analytics & Advertising

The free version may display contextual ads. We use Google AdMob for ad serving; AdMob's data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers see no ads. We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs.

5. Data Storage & Security

All your financial data is stored in Apple's SwiftData container on your device. It is protected by your device's built-in security (Face ID / Touch ID / passcode). We do not have access to your data, and iCloud sync is not currently enabled.

6. Children's Privacy

Sripada Expense Buddy is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
